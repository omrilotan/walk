Privacy notice

Version 2026-11-04

Without an account, nothing leaves your device. Walk runs in your browser and keeps your walks there.

With an account (optional), we keep your email address and your walk totals, so they sync between your browsers and reach the AI assistants you connect. Nothing else. No names, no passwords, no location, no IP addresses, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who we are

Walk is run by Omri Lotan, an individual in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of the data described here. Contact: support@omrilotan.com.

What we store, and why

Only when you sign in. Each item is kept only as long as it's needed.

Accounts with nothing synced and no sign-in for two years are deleted automatically. Deleted data stays in the database's automatic backups for up to 30 days, then it's gone. We keep no request logs.

What stays on your device

Your walks, their per-second readings, your settings, the ID of your pad, and a random ID that ties a sign-in code to your browser. These stay in your browser's storage and never reach us. If you sign in, one cookie keeps you signed in. It's strictly necessary, so there's no cookie banner. We use no other cookies.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts Walk, its database and the sign-in emails, as our processor under its data processing terms. Your account and walks are stored in the European Union. Sign-in emails, and the records of assistants you connect, pass through Cloudflare's global network, with the safeguards its terms provide.
  • AI assistants you connect. When you connect one, it reads your synced walks on your behalf. What it does with them is up to its provider and your settings there. You choose whether to connect one, and can disconnect it any time.

Walk loads nothing from other websites: its fonts and icons are served by Walk itself.

Your rights

  • See and take your data: in Walk, Menu, Sync and assistants, Download my data.
  • Delete it: delete walks, use Reset my data, or Delete account. Deleting the account removes everything above.
  • Withdraw consent: sign out and delete your account, or disconnect an assistant. Walk keeps working on your device.
  • Correct it, restrict or object: email us.
  • Complain: to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), or the data protection authority where you live.

Security

Everything travels encrypted. Codes and sessions are stored only as hashes. Sign-in codes work only in the browser that asked for them, and a few times at most.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 16 and over.
