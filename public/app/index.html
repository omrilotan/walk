    Without an account This browser only

    An assistant in the same Chrome window runs the pad with you, through WebMCP.

    1. Use Chrome with an assistant that supports WebMCP.
    2. Connect your pad.
    3. Ask it to start, pause for a call, or pick up the pace.

    Pause and stop always work right away.

    About Walk

    Control your walking pad from the browser and see every walk you've taken. Works with Superun, DeerRun and other PitPat pads.

    • Private. Walks stay on this device. Cloud sync is optional.
    • Works offline. Install it from your browser.
    • Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer or Android. Not on iPhone.
    • Stay safe. Keep the pad's remote within reach.
