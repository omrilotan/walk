    About Walk

    Control your walking pad from the browser. Start, pause, stop and set your speed, and see every walk you've taken. Works with PitPat walking pads.

    • Private by design. Your walks are stored only on this device. No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking.
    • Instant. No app store and no sign-up. It runs in your browser.
    • Your data is yours. Clearing your browser's site data deletes your walks, so export a backup now and then. Import it on this device or another one.
    • Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer or an Android phone. iPhone, iPad, Safari and Firefox don't allow websites to use Bluetooth.
    • Works with AI agents. Walk supports WebMCP, a new browser standard that lets a web page offer its controls to an AI assistant in the same browser. Ask the assistant how your week went, to pause for a call, or to pick up the pace. It can read your walks, pause, stop and change speed. Starting the belt is your call: it asks you first, unless you choose to let it start on its own. Walk itself still sends nothing anywhere; what the assistant does with what it reads is up to the assistant you use. Chrome only, for now.
    • Stay safe. Walk controls moving equipment. Keep the pad's remote within reach.
